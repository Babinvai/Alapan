OUR BEGINNING

A Heritage Built on Milk.

For generations, the Ghosh name has been closely connected with milk and dairy in Krishnanagar. Growing up around the everyday details of good milk—its source, freshness, careful handling and familiar aroma—we came to value food that is made with patience and responsibility.

As food supply chains became longer and less visible, finding genuinely trustworthy ghee became harder. Ghosh Dhara began with a simple intention: to bring traditional ghee to modern homes with clear sourcing, careful handling and batch-level accountability.

Our promise is simple: honour traditional preparation while following disciplined quality practices.

— Alapan Ghosh, Ghosh Dhara
