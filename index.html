Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between cow ghee and buffalo ghee?

Cow ghee is typically golden yellow, lighter in texture, and easier to digest, making it ideal for daily consumption and Ayurvedic uses. Buffalo ghee is white, thicker, and often preferred for deep frying or making heavy sweets.

How should I store ghee? Does it need refrigeration?

Store your ghee in a cool, dark, and dry place away from direct sunlight. There is absolutely no need to refrigerate it! Always use a clean, dry spoon to ensure the ghee stays fresh for months.

Why can ghee sometimes become grainy (danedar)?

Our ghee is naturally grainy because we carefully slow-cook pure cream over a gentle flame. The slow crystallization of fats creates that authentic "danedar" texture that is the hallmark of premium quality ghee.

Are your other pantry items (Honey, Bori, Neem Dust) also pure?

Yes! Just like our ghee, our Sundarban Honey, sun-dried Bori, and Neem Leaf Dust are 100% natural, sourced directly from trusted villages, and completely free of any artificial preservatives or chemicals.

How do I check my batch details?

Every jar of Ghosh Dhara ghee comes with a unique batch number printed on the back label. You can use this number to trace the production date and verify the independent lab testing results for your specific jar.

What should I do if my jar is damaged or leaking?

We pack every jar with extreme care, but if your jar arrives damaged or leaking during transit, simply reach out to us within 24 hours with a photo. We will immediately arrange a replacement for you, no questions asked.
