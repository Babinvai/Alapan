Shipping & Returns

Clear, transparent, and fair policies for your peace of mind. Last updated: September 2026.

1. Shipping & Delivery

We deliver across India using trusted courier partners. Orders are typically dispatched within 1-2 business days.

  • Standard Delivery: 3-7 business days depending on your pin code.
  • Shipping Charges: Free shipping on orders above ₹999. A nominal fee applies to smaller orders.
  • Tracking: Once dispatched, a tracking link will be sent to your registered email or WhatsApp.

2. Returns & Refunds

Because ghee is a consumable food product, maintaining hygiene and safety is our absolute priority. We cannot accept returns on opened food items, as they cannot be resold.

However, we offer replacements or refunds under the following specific conditions:

  • The jar was damaged, broken, or leaking upon arrival.
  • You received the incorrect product or size.
  • The product is defective or compromised.

3. How to Request a Replacement

If you face any of the issues above, please follow these steps strictly within 24 to 48 hours of delivery:

  1. Do not discard the packaging or the product.
  2. Take clear photographs and/or a video showing the damage, leakage, or incorrect item.
  3. Send the photos along with your Order ID to our WhatsApp at +91 8653890554 or email us.

Our team will review the claim and initiate a free replacement or a full refund to your original payment method (or bank account for COD orders) within 3-5 business days.

4. Cancellations

You may cancel your order at any time before it has been dispatched from our facility. Once an order is handed over to the courier, it cannot be canceled.

We are available on WhatsApp to resolve any issues you might face with your order.
